# ME feature list

## Built (version 3.84.0)

**Account and sync**
- Sign in, or create an account (password typed twice, "Account created" screen), with Show password
- Account and sync status in Settings, with Sign out
- Data saved on the phone (works offline) and synced to Firebase across devices
- Export to CSV: workouts (with left/right reps), food log by meal, weigh-ins, check-ins
- Installs to the home screen on iPhone and Android, with a crossed-swords icon
- Hidden check-for-updates tap under the version number in Settings

**Today**
- Combat level card with crossed swords, in your chosen theme (tap for what's needed)
- Streak card: current streak, days to next milestone, this week (Sunday to Saturday), this month's calendar
- Best streak at the top of History → Workouts
- Tap any past day to set it to Rest or Off, or to log a workout for that date
- Daily check-in: Exercise (Push, Pull, Legs, Back, Forearms), Rest or Off
- Weigh-in card (yellow until logged, green after)
- Gear card: % of planned workouts done since you started, current tier and the next one
- Food summary for the day
- Magic card: supplements and medications, Morning and Night folded, one light on the card, timers on the card

**Workouts**
- Your routine loaded: Push, Pull, Legs, Back and Forearms, with your starting numbers
- Exercises grouped by muscle (Chest, Shoulders, Biceps, Back, Legs, Forearms)
- Weekly variations (Var 1 / Var 2): the app alternates each week and you can switch
- Notes on each exercise (setup, form cues, machine settings)
- A rep goal for each set, with sessions hit, last reps and the change (+1)
- Each exercise has its own rep range (for example 20 for forearms, 20–22 for calf raises)
- "Go up in weight" when every set has hit the top goal enough sessions in a row
- Left and right reps counted separately (forearms)
- Rest timer between sets (1 to 3 minutes, set in Settings) with a bell when rest is over
- Skill XP toast, earthquake shake and voice cheer when a set is done
- Cardio by minutes and optional miles
- Complete workout (green button): closes what you did, even if you skipped one, and shows the summary pop-up: workout number, exercises, total sets, rep goals reached, and a quote (405 quotes, none repeats until all are used). "I am stronger." logs it and goes Home

**Food**
- Add food: pick the meal, Scan barcode, Frequently used, Create item, and one search bar (Frequently used shows until you type)
- Logged today: every item with calories and macros; tap to change or remove
- Goal + Exercise − Food = Remaining; protein, carbs and fat with % of calories
- Workout calories estimated from how long you lifted and your bodyweight
- "Fuel for muscle growth" check on training days (protein, calories, carbs)
- Search the basic foods list (offline) and Open Food Facts (online); barcode scanner
- Frequently used foods and your own items (one-tap add)

**Targets**
- Calculator from sex, birthday, height, weight, goal (Cut, Clean bulk, Dirty bulk) and activity outside the gym (Mifflin-St Jeor), set for maximum muscle: Cut −500 cal / 1.2 g protein per lb, Clean bulk +400 / 1 g, Dirty bulk +750 / 1 g
- Settings save automatically as you change them

**History**
- Workouts by date and by exercise, best-weight chart, strength gained %
- Strength levels (Beginner to Elite) for bench, squat and deadlift
- Food day by day by meal; weight chart with 7-day, 30-day and total change

**Motivation**
- Combat level 3–126 (about 2 years at a steady pace), built from your skills; combat level-up pop-up and fanfare
- Streak milestones at 7, 15, 22 and 30 days (and every 30 after), with a horn
- Gear tiers (Bronze to Obsidian): research-based body changes at 2 weeks to 1 year,
  unlocked while you're at 75%+ of planned workouts, with their own sound
- Voice prompts for tiers, level-ups and milestones (drop clips in audio/voice),
  and voice cheers for sets (audio/cheers); only one pop-up or sound at a time

- Preview every sound, pop-up and gear tier from Settings

**Workout card**
- Notes box under the name (saves as you type), "Last workout: day, date", Weight / Reps / Goal boxes per set,
  "N of 4 workouts until rep increase", "Most recent variation: N"

**Food search**
- Live search that narrows as you type; natural units (pieces, cups, fl oz, grams, oz); clears after adding;
  Frequently used button

**Character**
- Combat level (3–126) from your skills, RuneScape formula: ¼ × (Defence + Hitpoints + ½ Discipline) + 0.325 × (Attack + Strength)
- Tap the combat card on Today: it flips to show skill levels gained since your last combat level, and what's still needed
- 10 skills, 1–99, RuneScape XP table: Attack (Pull reps), Strength (Push reps), Defence (Back reps), Hitpoints (all four days),
  Agility (Legs reps), Discipline (streak days), Cooking (days at protein target), Stamina (sets), Mastery (weight increases),
  Herblore (supplements taken)
- Each set shows the skill XP it earned and any skill level-ups
- Skill tiles with icons and yellow pixel numbers; tap to flip and see XP and XP to the next level
- Pixel-art character in your gear tier colours; tap for one of 100 random animations
- Supplements set up in Settings with suggested amounts from your details; daily check-off on the Food tab

**Look**
- Bottom bar: Today, Workout, Food, Character, History; swipe in from the left edge for Settings
- Themes: Parchment, Stone wall, Dark wood (the whole app follows the level card theme)
- Wall sconces either side of the Today title, with flames that change colour together (15-second loop)
