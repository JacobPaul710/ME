# ME feature list

## Built (version 2.13.0)

**Account and sync**
- Sign in, or create an account (password typed twice, "Account created" screen), with Show password
- Account button on Today with your email and Sign out
- Data saved on the phone (works offline) and synced to Firebase across devices
- Export to CSV: workouts, food log, weigh-ins, check-ins
- Installs to the home screen on iPhone and Android

**Today**
- Level card (1–99) with XP bar and crossed swords, in your chosen theme
- Streak card: current streak, best streak, days to next milestone, last 7 days, this month's calendar
- Tap any past day to set it to Rest or Off, or to log a workout for that date
- Daily check-in: Exercise (Push, Pull, Legs, Forearms; Back coming later), Rest or Off
- Weigh-in card (yellow until logged, green after)
- Consistency card: Push/Pull/Legs days in the last 30 vs a goal of 16
- Food summary for the day

**Workouts**
- Day types with saved exercises; add from a dropdown or create new (weights or cardio)
- Sets with weight and reps; yellow light turns green when a set is done
- Last result, last day of that type, rep goal (10 → 11 → 12) and sessions in a row
- "Go up in weight" prompt after hitting 12 reps 4 sessions in a row (number adjustable)
- XP toast, earthquake shake and voice cheer when a set is done
- Cardio by minutes and optional miles

**Food**
- Diary by meal: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snacks, with every item's calories and macros
- Calories remaining (Goal − Food = Remaining); protein, carbs and fat with % of calories
- Search the basic foods list (offline) and Open Food Facts (online)
- Barcode scanner
- Frequently used foods and custom items (one-tap add)
- Edit an item's amount or meal, or remove it

**History**
- Workouts by date and by exercise, best-weight chart, strength gained %
- Strength levels (Beginner to Elite) for bench, squat and deadlift
- Food day by day; weight chart with 7-day, 30-day and total change

**Motivation**
- Levels with XP; level-up pop-up
- Streak milestones at 7, 15, 22 and 30 days (pop-ups)
- Progress notes at days 7, 14, 28, 42, 56, 84, 182 and 365 while consistent

**Look**
- Themes: Parchment, Stone wall, Dark wood (whole app follows the level card theme)

## Planned (requested, not built yet)
- Level system rework: max level 126, XP that scales with the weight you lift, small bonus per completed set, new-weight bonus and streak milestones that scale with your level
- Real-life progress unlocks ("gear tiers"): body changes you should be able to see or measure after consistent training, shown as unlocks with an optional voice prompt
- Sound effects and voice prompts, spaced so they don't all fire at once
- Hidden "check for updates" tap under the version number
- Calories burned from workouts, added to the day's food goal, with a check that intake is enough for muscle growth
- Target calculator: sex, age, height, weight, goal weight and activity level generate your daily targets
- Your routine pre-loaded (Push, Pull, Legs, Forearms with muscle groups and variations)
- Notes on each exercise
- Rest timer between sets (1 to 3 minutes)
- Left/right reps for one-arm exercises
- Per-exercise rep range (for example 20 for forearms, 22 for calf raises)
- New home screen icon
- Basic foods list loaded (needs one command on your Mac)
