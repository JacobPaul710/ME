# ME changelog

Version numbers are MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: MAJOR = overhaul that breaks old data or setup,
MINOR = new feature, PATCH = bug fix or small tweak.

## 3.15.3 (ME patch 8)
- 3.15.3 Fire stands moved beside the level card, redrawn, and the colour cycle now works on iPhone; home screen icon files renamed so phones pick up the crossed swords.
- 3.15.2 Sync status and account moved from the top of Today into Settings → Account.
- 3.15.1 Activity levels described by your day, not by steps (six levels kept).

## 3.15.0 (ME patch 7)
- 3.15.0 Burning braziers either side of the Today title, flickering and slowly changing colour.
- 3.14.0 Activity outside the gym picked by your usual daily steps (six levels).
- 3.13.0 Goal picker replaces goal weight: Cut, Clean bulk or Dirty bulk, each with its own calories and protein.
- 3.12.0 Birthday replaces age, so your age updates by itself.

## 3.11.1 (ME patch 6)
- 3.11.1 New home screen icon: crossed swords.
- 3.11.0 Rest timer between sets (1 to 3 minutes, set on the Workout tab).
- 3.10.0 Back day turned on, and your routine loaded (Push, Pull, Legs, Back, Forearms) with your current numbers.
- 3.9.0 Muscle groups on workout days, and weekly variations (pick Var 1 or Var 2 each week).
- 3.8.0 Left and right reps counted separately (forearms).
- 3.7.0 Notes on each exercise.
- 3.6.0 A rep goal for each set, and each exercise's own rep range.
- 3.5.0 Workout calories added to the day's food goal, with a "Fuel for muscle growth" check.
- 3.4.0 Target calculator from your details (Mifflin-St Jeor).
- 3.3.0 Hidden check-for-updates tap under the version number.
- 3.2.0 Sound effects and voice prompts for level-ups, streak milestones and gear tiers, one at a time.
- 3.1.0 Gear tiers: real-life body changes unlocked by staying at 75%+ of planned workouts (replace progress notes).
- 3.0.0 Level system rework: levels 1 to 126 (about 2 years), XP that grows with the weight lifted, a small bonus per set, and bonuses that scale with your level.

## 2.13.0 (ME patch 5)
- 2.13.0 Voice cheers: a random clip from audio/cheers plays when you finish a set (on/off in Settings).
- 2.12.0 Earthquake: the screen shakes when you complete a set (Android phones also buzz).
- 2.11.0 The whole app follows the level card theme: colors, cards, buttons, background texture and headings.

## 2.10.0 (ME patch 4)
- 2.10.0 Level card themes: Parchment, Stone wall and Dark wood, picked at the bottom of Settings.

## 2.9.3 (ME patch 3)
- 2.9.3 Crossed swords on the level card.
- 2.9.2 Fix: the word "null" showed above the food card before your first workout.
- 2.9.1 Food card on Today is titled with the date.
- 2.9.0 Food tab becomes a diary: meals, every item's macros, calories remaining, % of calories.
- 2.8.0 Tap a past day to change it to Rest or Off, or to log a workout for that date.
- 2.7.0 Account button on Today with your email and Sign out.
- 2.6.0 Show password on sign-in and create-account screens.
- 2.5.0 Separate Create account screen with password confirmation and "Account created".

## 2.4.0 (ME patch 2)
- 2.4.0 Strength levels (Beginner to Elite) for bench, squat and deadlift.
- 2.3.0 Consistency card and progress notes (Push, Pull, Legs over the last 30 days).
- 2.2.0 Level 1 to 99 with XP and level-up pop-ups.
- 2.1.0 Streak card with best streak, countdown, week view and month calendar.
- 2.0.0 Overhaul: sync and sign-in moved from Supabase to Firebase (new accounts needed).

## 1.0.0 (ME patch 1)
- First release: workouts with rep goals, cardio, history and progress, food logging with
  search, barcode scan and saved foods, daily targets, weigh-ins, streak and milestones,
  offline use, CSV export.
