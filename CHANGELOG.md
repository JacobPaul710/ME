# ME changelog

Version numbers are MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: MAJOR = overhaul that breaks old data or setup,
MINOR = new feature, PATCH = bug fix or small tweak.

## 3.77.0 (ME patch 22)
- 3.77.0 Your Magic list is set up for you once: Creatine 2.5 g morning and night, Ritalin 27 mg (10 h timer), Ritalin 10 mg optional (4 h timer), Wellbutrin (morning), Lexapro (night).
- 3.76.0 Today order: combat level, calendar, Magic, weigh-in, "What are we doing today?", nutrition.
- 3.75.0 Exercises close only when you tap Done, never on their own.
- 3.74.0 Every food has a "whole" option and never asks you to type a size (package size from the data, or one serving); every drink gets swallows, cups and fl oz.
- 3.73.0 Each meal is one group with a yellow outline (header and entries together), entries without their own boxes.
- 3.72.2 Pop-ups always sit in the middle of the screen.
- 3.72.1 Check-box rows in Settings (rest timer, optional) read left to right.

## 3.72.0 (ME patch 21)
- 3.72.0 Magic card on Today: Morning and Night check-offs with a wand by each, Morning folds when done and Night opens, timers (e.g. 10 h after Ritalin) count down right on the card, optional items give bonus XP.
- 3.71.0 Supplements and medications: each has When (Morning, Night, or both), Optional, and an optional timer, set in Settings. They moved off the Food tab onto the Magic card.
- 3.70.0 Herblore is now Magic, with a wizard hat icon.

## 3.69.0 (ME patch 20)
- 3.69.0 New app logo: walnut dumbbell with ME.
- 3.68.0 Every weighed food has a "whole item" choice; if its size isn't known you type it once and it's remembered.
- 3.67.0 Meals have coloured headers with icons; your logged food glows so it never looks like a button.
- 3.66.0 Workout summary card when every exercise is done: exercises, weight lifted, XP, rep goals reached, weight increases earned, and "Push workout number N…".
- 3.65.0 A check box on the left of each set, and a "Done" button under the last set of every exercise.
- 3.64.3 Rest timer is off by default (switch in Settings).
- 3.64.2 Starting fresh: rep goals and "N of 4" counts only count workouts logged in the app (the spreadsheet only fills in starting weights and reps).

## 3.64.1 (ME patch 19)
- 3.64.1 When rest ends and you're on another tab, a message tells you it's time for your next set.
- 3.64.0 Barcode scanner: sharper camera, continuous focus, reads a zoomed strip in the aim box about 10 times a second, and a flashlight button where the phone allows it.
- 3.63.1 Gear is in the icebox: no gear card on Today and no gear pop-ups; the knight wears plain clothes.
- 3.63.0 Level-up pop-ups you tap OK to close: a grand one for combat levels and a smaller one for each skill level. Pop-ups wait their turn.
- 3.62.0 Supplements can be taken more than once a day, each dose its own check-off with the time you took it.
- 3.61.0 Food units: count by the piece whenever the serving is a count (cookies, nuggets…), "whole can / package" when the size is known, cups from the label, and "swallows" (about 25 ml) for drinks.
- 3.60.0 Add food to an earlier day: "Other day" on the Food tab, or "Food for this day" on any calendar day.
- 3.59.0 The Workout tab takes you back to the workout you're doing (the picker only when you haven't picked one).
- 3.58.1 "N of 4 workouts until rep increase" can no longer go past 4.

## 3.45.0 (ME patch 15)
- 3.45.0 Characters: your pick locks in and you can name it. Switch back to a paused one any time. A heart floats up when you touch it; idles and tap animations last about twice as long, one every 4–6 seconds. Each monster and the knight has its own quiet backdrop. Tidefang removed (8 monsters).

- 3.43.0 Weekly recap: on Mondays, Today shows last week (workouts, sets, weight lifted, combat and skill levels, streak days, average calories and protein, weight change). Past weeks in History → Weeks.
- 3.42.3 Rest-between-sets dropdown no longer overlaps its label in Settings.
- 3.42.2 Back of the combat card: just "Still needed for combat level N" and the options (the Done list is gone).
- 3.42.1 The combat level number on the card uses the pixel font (same colour).
