# ME changelog

Version numbers are MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH: MAJOR = overhaul that breaks old data or setup,
MINOR = new feature, PATCH = bug fix or small tweak.

## 2.10.0 (ME patch 4)
- 2.10.0 Level card themes: Parchment, Stone wall and Dark wood, picked at the bottom of Settings.

## 2.9.3 (ME patch 3)
- 2.9.3 Crossed swords on the level card.
- 2.9.2 Fix: the word "null" showed above the food card before your first workout.
- 2.9.1 Food card on Today is titled with the date.
- 2.9.0 Food tab becomes a diary: meals, every item's macros, calories remaining, % of calories.
- 2.8.0 Tap a past day to change it to Rest or Off, or to log a workout for that date.
- 2.7.0 Account button on Today with your email and Sign out.
- 2.6.0 Show password on sign-in and create-account screens.
- 2.5.0 Separate Create account screen with password confirmation and "Account created".

## 2.4.0 (ME patch 2)
- 2.4.0 Strength levels (Beginner to Elite) for bench, squat and deadlift.
- 2.3.0 Consistency card and progress notes (Push, Pull, Legs over the last 30 days).
- 2.2.0 Level 1 to 99 with XP and level-up pop-ups.
- 2.1.0 Streak card with best streak, countdown, week view and month calendar.
- 2.0.0 Overhaul: sync and sign-in moved from Supabase to Firebase (new accounts needed).

## 1.0.0 (ME patch 1)
- First release: workouts with rep goals, cardio, history and progress, food logging with
  search, barcode scan and saved foods, daily targets, weigh-ins, streak and milestones,
  offline use, CSV export.
